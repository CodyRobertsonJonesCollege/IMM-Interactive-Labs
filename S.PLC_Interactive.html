Reference: essential ladder instructions

Symbol / AB nameMeaning
—| |— / XICTrue when the referenced bit is 1 (Examine If Closed).
—|/|— / XIOTrue when the referenced bit is 0 (Examine If Open).
—( )— / OTESets or clears a bit based on rung continuity (Output Energize).
TONOn-delay timer: done after the rung stays true for the preset duration.
CTUCount-up: increments on a false-to-true rung transition.

XIC and XIO test logical bit states; they do not directly describe whether a physical field contact is normally open or normally closed. Real PLC implementations vary by platform and configuration.
